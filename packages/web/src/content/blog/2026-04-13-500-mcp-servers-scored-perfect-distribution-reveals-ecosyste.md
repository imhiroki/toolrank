**Scenario 2: Scoring Saturation**
Current scoring may not differentiate effectively among competent implementations. When Microsoft Learn MCP, Docfork variants, and specialized tools like Pest Sentinel all score identically, the framework might need recalibration to capture nuanced quality differences.
